Over two action-packed days, student visionaries, programmers, and designers collaborate to engineer working prototypes addressing critical real-world challenges.

03 // EVENT TIMELINE

24-HOUR HACKATHON SCHEDULE

Two days of intense building, continuous mentorship, review checkpoints, and high-stakes jury pitching.

08:30 AM - 09:30 AM

Participant Registration & Hack Desk Setup

Team check-in, hack badge & kit distribution, Wi-Fi onboarding, and workstation allocation at KPRIET.

09:30 AM - 10:15 AM

Inaugural Ceremony & Keynote Address

Welcome address by dignitaries from the Department of AI & Data Science and ACM Student Chapter, accompanied by an inspiring industry keynote.

10:15 AM - 10:30 AM

Problem Statement Briefing & Hack Kickoff

Official release of track parameters, evaluation rubric, and start of the 24-hour countdown clock.

10:30 AM - 01:00 PM

Sprint 1: Ideation & Architectural Prototyping

Teams brainstorm problem mapping, wireframing, tech stack finalization, and initial repository scaffolding.

01:00 PM - 02:00 PM

Buffet Lunch & Peer Networking

Buffet lunch break and networking opportunity with fellow innovators and mentors.

02:00 PM - 04:30 PM

Sprint 2: Core Development Phase

Building core module logic, training predictive/AI models, writing backend services, and designing front-end interfaces.

04:30 PM - 05:30 PM

Mentorship Checkpoint 1: Architecture Review

Industry experts and faculty mentors visit team bays to review system designs and offer actionable optimization tips.

05:30 PM - 07:30 PM

Sprint 3: System Integration & Testing

Integrating APIs, connecting databases, executing hardware sensors, and refining UI/UX responsiveness.

07:30 PM - 08:30 PM

Dinner Break & Energy Recharge

Hot dinner served in the campus dining hall to re-energize developers for the night sprint.

08:30 PM - 10:30 PM

Mentorship Checkpoint 2: Mid-Hack Evaluation

Mid-hack jury assessment of working prototype components, feasibility, and alignment with the track objectives.

10:30 PM - Overnight

Overnight Coding Sprint & Midnight Lounge

Midnight refreshments, energizing coffee/tea, gaming mini-activities, and continuous mentor desk support throughout the night.

06:30 AM - 07:30 AM

Morning Refreshments & Breakfast

Morning breakfast and hot beverages at the campus cafeteria to gear up for finale pitches.

07:30 AM - 08:30 AM

Final Polish & Code Freeze

Final code commits, repository pushes, presentation slide creation, and demo rehearsals.

08:30 AM - 10:30 AM

Preliminary Pitching & Jury Evaluation

Track-wise project pitches, live demo presentations, code inspections, and Q&A by distinguished judges.

10:30 AM - 11:30 AM

Grand Finale: Top 10 Stage Demos

Shortlisted finalists present live product demonstrations on the main auditorium stage before the grand jury panel.

11:30 AM - 12:30 PM

Valedictory Ceremony, Internship Awards & Prize Distribution

Declaration of winners, awarding the ₹25,000 cash prizes, presentation of exclusive internship offers, and felicitation of participants.

12:30 PM Onwards

Group Photo, Lunch & Departure

Commemorative photo sessions, lunch, and official wrap-up of HackNexus’27.
